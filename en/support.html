Support

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Write to us with problems, suggestions or subscription questions. Please do not include your birth details; they are not needed to understand the issue.

Contact

antares.iosapp@gmail.com

Do I need an account?

No. ANTARES has no accounts; everything is calculated and stored on your device.

Is my data moved to another device?

No. Records are kept only on this device and are not synced to iCloud.

Which system is used?

Tropical zodiac and Whole Sign houses, with the historical time zone and daylight saving time of the birthplace.

I don't know my birth time.

Planets are calculated approximately; the ascendant and houses depend on the birth time and are not shown.

What does ANTARES PRO unlock?

Detailed readings, the Reading tab of your birth chart, this week's and upcoming transits, and Ask the Sky.

Why don't I see a free trial?

The 3-day trial depends on App Store eligibility and can be used once per subscription group.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions, or in the app: Profile > ANTARES PRO > "Manage Apple subscriptions".

PRO is missing on my new phone.

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account and tap "Restore Purchases" on the ANTARES PRO screen.

How do I delete my data?

Profile > Your Data > "Delete all my data". Your subscription is managed in your Apple Account.
