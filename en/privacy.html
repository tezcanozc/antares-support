Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

ANTARES runs astrological calculations on your device. The app does not send us your birth details, saved records or questions you enter in Ask the Sky.

Privacy and support contact

antares.iosapp@gmail.com

Information you enter

Your name (optional), birth date, time and place are used only to calculate your chart. This information, your saved charts, Sky Journal notes and app settings are stored in your device's Keychain as this-device-only items. They are not synced to iCloud and never reach us.

Ask the Sky

Your question is processed in device memory by an on-device rule engine only while the screen is open. It is not stored or sent, and it is cleared when you leave the screen.

Notifications

If you turn on event reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Permission is requested only when you turn a reminder on. No remote (push) notifications are sent.

Sharing

A chart image is sent only when you tap Share, to the app you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Subscription and payment

ANTARES PRO purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information. The app reads your subscription status only from Apple-verified transactions on the device.

Optional sign-in

You can create an ANTARES account with Sign in with Apple. Firebase Authentication processes the account ID, email address and display name, when available, in the United States to verify sign-in and manage your account. Apple provides a relay address if you choose to hide your email. Firebase also processes IP addresses and client information for security and abuse prevention, and keeps logged IP addresses for a few weeks.

Location and tracking

You choose the birthplace manually. The app does not access device location. The app does not use advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile > Data and Privacy > My Accounts and choose Delete my account. Firebase removes account information from live and backup systems within 180 days of deletion. Use Your Data to erase records saved on your device. Manage your subscription in your Apple Account.

Deleting your data

Profile > Your Data > "Delete all my data" deletes your profile, saved charts, Sky Journal notes and event reminders in one step. Your language preference is kept. Purchases made through Apple and your subscription are not affected. iOS may keep Keychain items after the app is deleted, so we recommend deleting your data in the app before removing it.

Support email

If you choose to email us, your email address and message reach our support inbox. Please leave birth details out of your message.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page with an updated date.
